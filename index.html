How the Plinko board works

The Plinko board is a triangle of pins arranged in rows. When the Plinko ball hits each peg, it can move left or right, and that small decision repeats all the way down. The path is random, so no player can force the ball into one pocket or another. That is why the game looks simple but still has a strong gambling appeal for people who like fast rounds.

Each pocket at the bottom has its own multiplier, and the board usually pays less in the middle and more on the outer edges. That structure is why Plinko casino fans often talk about balance, risk, and variance. The maths behind it is straightforward: common landings pay less, rare landings pay more, and the house keeps a margin inside the payout table.
